🎤 Voice Training

Hear a note, sing it back, and the dial shows if you're flat or sharp.

How to use it
  1. Pick an octave that feels comfortable: Low, Medium or High.
  2. Chromatic is set for you. It lists every note, so you can learn them all. Pick a Start note to begin anywhere. Want a specific scale? Choose Major, Minor or Pentatonic instead.
  3. Tap a note to hear it. Allow the microphone when your phone asks.
  4. Sing the note you heard. If the needle leans left you're flat, so go higher. If it leans right you're sharp, so go lower.
  5. Keep the needle in the green for one second to earn a ✓ on that note.

Tip: headphones work best, and Play scale lets you hear the whole scale first.
